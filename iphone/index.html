Clocked Download

Start on your Mac

Your iPhone can tell Clocked the moment you open a distracting app, so the clock stops straight away. It works over your Wi-Fi, and nothing goes over the internet.

  1. Open Clocked on your Mac.
  2. Go to Settings → Accountability → iPhone and click Set Up.
  3. Scan the code it shows with your iPhone's camera. This page opens with your own links and you can carry on from there.

You need Clocked, and your iPhone and Mac on the same Wi-Fi.

Add “Clocked App Opened”

Tap the button. It copies your link and opens the shortcut. When Shortcuts asks for a link, paste it, then tap Add Shortcut.

Add “Clocked App Closed”

Same again. Tap, then paste and Add Shortcut.

Copy link and add shortcut

When an app opens

Now tell your iPhone when to run it.

  1. Open Shortcuts and go to the Automation tab.
  2. Tap +, then App.
  3. Choose the apps that distract you and tick Is Opened.
  4. Choose Run Immediately and turn off Notify When Run.
  5. Tap Next, then pick Clocked App Opened.
Open Shortcuts

When it closes

One more, the same way.

  1. In Automation, tap +, then App.
  2. Choose the same apps and tick Is Closed.
  3. Choose Run Immediately and turn off Notify When Run.
  4. Tap Next, then pick Clocked App Closed.
Open Shortcuts

Only while studying (optional)

If you use a Study Focus during sessions, you can make the shortcuts send nothing the rest of the time. Do this for both shortcuts:

  1. Open the shortcut in Shortcuts.
  2. Add Get Current Focus at the top.
  3. Add If below it: Current Focus is Study.
  4. Drag URL and Get Contents of URL inside the If.

Skip this if you don't use a Study Focus. Clocked ignores messages outside a session anyway.

Try it

Open one of your apps on this iPhone. Clocked's setup on your Mac shows a tick when the message arrives.

That's it. During a session, opening those apps stops the clock straight away.
