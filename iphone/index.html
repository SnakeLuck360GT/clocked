Clocked Download

iPhone link

Your iPhone tells Clocked the moment you open a distracting app, so the clock stops straight away. It works over your Wi-Fi, and nothing goes over the internet.

  1. On your Mac, open Clocked and turn on Settings → Accountability → iPhone. Keep it open: it shows two links you'll paste in the next step.
  2. On your iPhone, open this page in Safari and add both shortcuts. Each one asks for a link: paste the matching one from Clocked. Copying on your Mac and pasting on your iPhone works if Handoff is on; otherwise AirDrop them.
    Clocked App Opened Clocked App Closed
  3. In Shortcuts, go to Automation, tap +, and choose App. Pick the apps that distract you, tick Is Opened, choose Run Immediately and turn off Notify When Run. For the action, choose Run Shortcut → Clocked App Opened.
  4. Make a second automation the same way with Is Closed and Clocked App Closed.

Your iPhone and Mac need to be on the same Wi-Fi. Messages only count while a session is running. Clocked only accepts messages with your pairing key, and New Pairing Key in Settings makes the old links stop working.
