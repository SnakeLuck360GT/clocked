Clocked Download

Can't focus while you study?

Clocked is a study timer for the Mac that stops counting the moment you pick up your phone, open Instagram or walk away.

Download for Mac

Version 3.0. Requires macOS 26 on Apple Silicon.

Your phone

Private by design

Clocked has no internet access.

The app that uses your camera and microphone isn't allowed to connect to anything. macOS's sandbox blocks it, because the network permission was never given to the app. That's how it's built, not a setting you could switch on by mistake.

Check it yourself in Terminal:

codesign -d --entitlements - /Applications/Clocked.app

You'll see the camera, the microphone and the sandbox, and no network.client.

  • Only during a session. The camera and mic switch on when you press play and off when you stop.
  • Nothing is recorded. Each frame and sound is analysed in memory and thrown away.
  • Almost nothing is saved. Settings, daily totals and a few numbers about your posture. No images, audio, keystrokes or page titles.
  • Study groups are separate. They're off until you turn them on, and they run in a small helper inside the app, ClockedSync, that can reach the internet but can't use the camera or mic. It only gets your totals after they're encrypted on your Mac, and the invite code is the key, so the server can't read them. 
    codesign -d --entitlements - /Applications/Clocked.app/Contents/XPCServices/ClockedSync.xpc

Install

  1. Drag Clocked into Applications.
  2. The first time, open System Settings → Privacy & Security and click Open Anyway.
